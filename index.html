06 / Destination Comparison

Two Five-Star Breaks, Two Different Experiences

Snapper Rocks and Mystics both achieve 5.0/5 overall among the selected destinations receiving at least 15 votes. Yet their identical overall scores conceal important differences. Comparing individual attributes reveals why the highest-rated destination is not necessarily the best match for every surfer.

Although Snapper Rocks and Mystics have equally strong overall ratings, their appeal cannot be explained by ratings alone. Surfing conditions also vary throughout the year. Comparing these highly rated destinations with the Bilinga area provides a seasonal perspective on the diversity of Australia's surf breaks.
